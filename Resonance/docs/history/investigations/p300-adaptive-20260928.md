# Adaptive P300 cycle stopping

Study date: 2026-09-28. Delivered as an optional native Typing setting, off by default. [Current contract](../../components/p300.md#adaptive-typing) · [Implementation evidence](../changes/2026-09-28-p300-adaptive.md).

## Recommendation and results

Use a stable row/column lead with minimum **two scored cycles**, threshold **1.5**, and the existing cycle count as the hard maximum. No new classifier fit or probability calibration is required. This is a scale-normalized heuristic, not a 95% confidence rule. Retrospective results justify optional human testing, not enabling it by default.

Q8 and Q12 are the same 43 people, not independent cohorts. Movicom has 49 year/subject groups in each matrix, with 686 and 623 eligible learning letters. Across personal/group0/group2 there are 6,109 method–letter cases, not independent observations.

The final all-learning threshold was 1.5. Of 48 outer folds, 47 also selected 1.5; the Movicom-2025-held-out fold selected 1.0. On the whole learning corpus, fixed 1.5 gives 653/686 in 3×3 and 606/623 in 6×6, saving 14.61% and 15.70%. These are **development** figures. The less favorable 649/686 above evaluates the complete threshold-selection procedure and should not be replaced by its more favorable development result.

## Algorithm and validation

For each completed scored cycle, center row scores across rows and column scores across columns. At k cycles, calculate each group's mean and pool residual squared deviations from these means. Divide by `2*(k-1)*(m-1)`, where m is matrix width, giving v. Divide each top-minus-runner-up axis lead by `sqrt(2*v/k)`. Evidence is the smaller of the two values. A relative variance floor prevents zero division. Both axes must have positive leads, evidence must meet the threshold, k must meet the minimum, and the leading cell must match the preceding cumulative-cycle winner.

The reference search compared this residual estimate with total centered spread, minimums 2/3, thresholds 0, .5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 15, and practical no-stop 1e6. It selected only on personal-model learning LOLO results: at most 1% newly wrong letters per source dataset relative to five cycles, then maximum equal-dataset average saving. Ties favored the larger threshold and minimum. Outer folds excluded a Movicom year across both matrices or a Q participant across both montages. Group models were stress tests and were never used to choose the stopping threshold. Personal classifier fits excluded the entire evaluated letter. Group fits excluded the participant; adaptation used the first two labelled letters, then tested later letters.

Current causal features and centered count-pooled Fisher fits were retained: Movicom3 HP2 .1/LP4 18 Hz, .001 ridge; Movicom6 and Q8 HP2 .3/LP4 12 Hz, .003; Q12 HP2 .5/LP4 12 Hz, .003. Windows are 100–600 ms with 40 ms means every 20 ms. Q12 uses the fixed deployed recipe and .25/.25 mean/covariance adaptation; its all-other-subject prior is recomputed within subject exclusion. Previously verified 8-channel source-excluding group fits were reused.

The initial protocol listed thresholds starting at 1.5. Before reading play, the learning search was expanded to include 0/.5/1 and rerun. That changed one outer fold and the reported nested figures, but not the final 1.5 rule. `protocol.json` is retained as originally written; `protocol-final.json` records the actual grid and this revision. This was a learning-only research search, not a preregistered trial.

## Availability and limitations

Presentation remains continuous. Evidence arrives only after full EEG epochs; the window checks at complete physical-cycle boundaries, usually one cycle later. The final decision includes all physically shown cycles. It can differ from the triggering leader. Simulation nominally allows 600 ms epoch plus 50 ms delivery, with the visible boundary at last onset +100 ms; sensitivity uses 600/650/850/1200 ms score availability. Production uses actual readiness.

Estimated time is first onset to decision, excluding cues, feedback, key waiting and corrections. Replay cannot remove overlapping EEG responses to future flashes present in the original recording, or predict changes in attention after earlier feedback. Earlier recipe optimization, cross-year identity uncertainty and Q montage overlap remain limitations. The empirical new-error bound is not an individual accuracy guarantee. Weak group models save less and can still stop confidently on a wrong letter.

Next step: alternate fixed/adaptive short blocks with the same maximum, timing, montage and synchronization, logging intended text, correctness, cycles, actual decision time and corrections. Confirm over more than one session before changing defaults. No human online accuracy or physical display timing was measured here.

Primary feasibility reference: [Mainsah et al. 2015, dynamic stopping in an ALS BCI study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4631027/). Their Bayesian likelihood method and results are not claimed as validation of this different heuristic.
