# P300 follow-up tuning on learning recordings

Date: 2026-09-26. Executed numerical research; no service, MATLAB fitter or distribution model changed.

Scope: 98 learning recordings, 49 per matrix; 623 letters in 6×6 and 686 in 3×3. Same retained pool as the [first tuning study](p300-learn-tuning-20260925.md). Whole-letter LOOCV stays within each recording. No play signal, target labels or scoring is used.

Artifacts: [illustrated report](../../../outputs/p300-followup-20260926/p300-followup-report.docx), [XLSX tables](../../../outputs/p300-followup-20260926/p300-followup-results.xlsx), [complete evidence](../../../src/_test-output/p300-followup-20260926/completed.json).

These are exploratory maxima selected by equally weighted recording/subject mean cycle-five accuracy, then pooled accuracy and earlier cycles. They are not independent test accuracy. Paired subject-bootstrap intervals are conditional on selected settings, without multiplicity correction.

The complete joint grid has 5,400 settings per matrix: low cutoffs 0.1/0.3/0.5/1/1.5/2 Hz, high 8/10/12/15/18/20 Hz, FIR 1023/2047 taps, direct Butterworth total order 4/8 or separate HP2+LP4, six windows (0–600, 100–600, 200–600, 0–700, 100–700, 150–700 ms), and ridge 0.001/0.003/0.01/0.03/0.1. All use 50 Hz features and causal anti-alias filtering.

The feature/covariance stage has 1,080 settings per matrix. It retains two best distinct filter/windows per family plus each prior winner as an interaction control. Feature options are point samples, 20/40/60 ms sliding means at 50 Hz, and nonoverlapping 40/60 ms bins with anti-alias filters designed for 25/16.67 Hz. Trailing partial bins are not used.

Centered n−1 covariance remains the basis. Pooling is either equal class contribution or weighting by n−1 degrees of freedom. Scalar ridge uses five strengths; diagonal shrinkage uses 0.01/0.03/0.1/0.3. OAS is estimated separately per class using the simplified large-p formula, with n−1 covariance scale retained. No baseline subtraction, fitted channel scaling or channel deletion is used.

All six selected family/matrix settings use count-weighted centered covariance and 0.001–0.003 scalar ridge. The four IIR settings use 40 ms sliding means; the FIR settings retain point samples. A boundary check at ridge 0.0001 and 0.0003 on three lower-bound winners did not improve them. OAS and diagonal shrinkage did not win the final comparisons.

The 108-candidate bank was frozen before this study’s results. Each outer letter is absent from all inner fits and all parameter/stopping choices. Two objectives are evaluated separately: final-cycle and mean cycles 1–4 accuracy. Each excluded pair serves the two ordered inner/outer directions. The first fixed bank candidate scores 604/623 and 638/686; nested choices show no consistent advantage.

The bank covers 0.3–15 Hz FIR1023/direct IIR4/HP2+LP4, 0–600 or 100–700 ms, point/mean20/mean40 features, equal/count ridge 0.003/0.01, equal diagonal 0.1 and equal OAS. It was informed by prior work on this corpus; nested evaluation is not a new independent dataset.

Stopping requires two consecutive equal winners and a normalized top-two score margin. The threshold is selected on inner predictions from 0.25/0.5/0.75/1/1.5/2/fixed-five, minimizing cycles subject to inner accuracy at least matching fixed-five. Held-out stopping loses 15/23 correct letters versus the same models at five cycles. This is a speed–accuracy tradeoff, not an accepted default.

## Quality and parameter stability

Quality rules are fixed in advance on the old winners. Train-only median/MAD of per-channel log RMS sets smooth high-energy weights. Weighted centered covariance and exposure-normalized weighted cell scores are tested separately and together. All letters remain in the denominator. Effects are mixed: FIR scoring adds one 6×6 letter; combined HP/LP weighting adds three 3×3 letters and loses one 6×6 letter.

A separate diagnostic uses epoch SD after common FIR1023 0.3–15 Hz plus 50 Hz AA over 0–700 ms. Large excursions overlap between correct and wrong letters. Raw channel range, variance and repeated-value fractions are retained; these do not establish physical saturation or artifact causes.

Other-year parameter selection uses the complete joint grid: choose from four years and score the choice on the fifth, retaining within-recording LOOCV classifier fitting. It does not validate the later globally shortlisted feature stage.

## Filtering and verification

Continuous causal zero-state filtering precedes epoch extraction. No filtfilt or per-epoch filter is used. FIR alignment requires (taps−1)/2 future samples; its wait is 1.022 or 2.046 seconds at 500 Hz. Epoch endpoint adds to that wait. The common elliptic AA stage has 0.1 dB ripple, 60 dB attenuation, pass edge 0.4 × feature rate and stop edge 0.5 × feature rate. IIR chronological state persists across letters. Recorded timing estimates are not a native SPEED or live typing benchmark.

All 294 inputs were hash-verified before and after work. Control parity covers 19,635 predictions in each of two paths. Independent verification covers 3,927 finalist fits, 2,618 nested outer fits, 2,618 inner fits, and every one of 1,309 nested selection/stopping decisions. 13,209 fixed-setting rows were reconciled against individual predictions. Rendered Word pages and all workbook views were inspected; saved workbook values were checked independently.
