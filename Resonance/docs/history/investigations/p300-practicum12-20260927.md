## Corpus and protocol

The [self-contained clinical12 subset](../../../equipment/Movicom/classifier-reference/corpus-clinical12/README.md) retains twelve raw continuous columns, original photo/blocks/messages and all 430 learning attempts from **43 Q subjects**. The previous optical-onset indices and five excluded letters are preserved byte-for-byte, leaving **425 letters**, five cycles each. Existing eight-channel samples agree exactly. Raw HDF hashes were verified before and after extraction. No play file was opened, used for selection, or evaluated.

Freeze nine nearby candidates before this run: baseline .3–12 Hz / 100–600 ms / .003 ridge; change only ridge to .01 or .03, HP to .1 or .5 Hz, LP to 10 or 15 Hz, start to 0 ms, or end to 700 ms. All use the existing centered count-pooled Fisher method, HP2 + LP4, zero-state continuous causal filtering, elliptic anti-alias 20/25 Hz (0.1 dB/60 dB), and 40 ms forward means every 20 ms. No filter is applied independently to an epoch and no filtfilt is used.

Outer leave-one-subject-out excludes the test participant entirely. Within the other 42, leave-one-subject-out priors fit 41 subjects and choose the recipe on zero-calibration letter accuracy. Objective: mean subject accuracy after five cycles, then mean over cycles 1–5, then frozen candidate order. For each requested short-calibration length, source-only inner folds then choose the existing mean/covariance blend grid using only letters after that prefix. The outer prior fits all 42 source participants. The held subject contributes only its first K labelled letters when adaptation is evaluated. Each subject's covariance is centered within class, pooled by degrees of freedom, divided by its scalar mean diagonal; its mean difference is divided by the square root of that scalar. Normalized moments are averaged equally across source subjects. Zero calibration uses no target-derived statistics.

The final deployable recipe is chosen by all-source LOSO development scores, its blend policy by the same source-only LOSO procedure, then its moments are fitted to all 43 subjects. This final all-source model is not evaluated as if its training participants were unseen. Nested results below estimate the selection procedure. Earlier research already used Q when developing the starting recipe, so this does not remove all historical researcher-selection optimism.

## Results

At five cycles, matched zero-calibration comparison on all 425 letters:

| Group classifier | Correct | Accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|
| Existing8, fixed current recipe, held-subject fit | 365/425 | 85.9% |
| Baseline12, same recipe, held-subject fit | 375/425 | 88.2% |
| Tuned12, fully nested selection | 380/425 | 89.4% |

Adding channels accounts for ten additional correct letters; tuning adds another five. The paired subject-mean improvement over Existing8 is **3.51 percentage points**, approximate subject-bootstrap 95% interval **1.40–5.87**. Tuning alone improves the subject mean by **1.16 points**, interval **−0.23–3.02**: the small tuning gain is uncertain. HP .5 Hz is selected in 40/43 outer folds, LP15 in two, start0 in one. The final deployment chooses HP .5 Hz. Its development LOSO total also happens to be 380/425; this equality does not make it independent evaluation.

## Frozen deployed recipe

HP2 **0.5 Hz**, LP4 **12 Hz**, antialias as above; **100–600 ms** optical epochs; **40 ms** means every **20 ms**; **24 features/channel, 288 total**, channel-major; count-pooled centered Fisher with **0.3%** mean-diagonal ridge and zero bias. Mean/covariance blend anchors: one letter **.25/.25**, two **.25/.25**, three **.5/.25**, four **.5/.25**, six **.5/.5**. Other counts use the existing interpolation/pseudo-count extension, not new measured optimal values.

Training corpus uses the existing validated optical indices. Live recipe uses **Photodiode**, rising **−1.85 V**, offset **0**, search **100 ms**. Optical detector compatibility, electrode placement and reference still need checking on the on-site rig. The coefficient/feature wait ends 600 ms after the aligned flash; no FIR look-ahead is introduced.
