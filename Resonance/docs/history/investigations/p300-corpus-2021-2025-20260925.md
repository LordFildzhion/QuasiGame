Scope: offline numerical research using retained 2021–2025 Movicom 6×6/3×3 recordings. No device, display, MATLAB process, deployed service or production model was changed.

## Protocol

Exactly the earlier 20 configurations: original Movicom 2,047-tap FIR, 1–10 Hz, with 1,023-sample alignment shift; causal fourth-order Butterworth SOS IIR, 1.5–10 Hz; 1-second/20-Hz and 0.6-second/50-Hz feature layouts; Mov0, Mov1, Mat0, Mat1 and Mat2. Channel-major features follow continuous filtering. FIR/IIR differ in bandwidth and phase/delay as well as filter family; this is a comparison of the two existing pipelines, not an isolated filter-order experiment.

Historical fitter definitions are unchanged: Mov0 is centered n−1 covariance without ridge; Mov1 adds 1% diagonal ridge. Mat0 is uncentered n second moments with 1% ridge, Mat1 removes its ridge, and Mat2 adds class-wise centering while retaining n denominators. Movicom variants normalize weights and subtract a midpoint bias. The updated live MATLAB centered n−1 default has the same discriminant direction as Mov1, not historical Mat0.

Learning validation leaves one complete letter out within its recording, fits all cycles of the other letters, and evaluates cumulative cycles 1–5. No parameter selection occurs inside these fixed runs. Summaries contain pooled letter accuracy, equal-weight subject means and exploratory 95% percentile intervals from 20,000 paired subject resamples (seed 93025). Resampling units are year/subject IDs; cross-year person identity is unknown. Intervals are not adjusted for trying multiple configurations.

Separate-recording replay freezes the training model before loading play EEG, resets filter state and evaluates all 100 explicit within-subject/matrix pairs. Three-cycle sessions produce only three cumulative results. Saved selected characters establish historical predictions, never intended targets. Initially, the scored subset contained the user-confirmed 165 attempts in ten 2025 6×6 sessions. It was subsequently expanded below without refitting. Participant 08 stays excluded.

## Findings

At five cycles, the highest pooled 6×6 CV score is FIR, 0.6 seconds/50 Hz, Mat2: **580/623 (93.1%)**. Mov1 is one letter behind, **579/623 (92.9%)**. For 3×3, IIR, 1 second/20 Hz, Mov1 and Mat2 both score **626/686 (91.3%)**.

Regularization is the clearer effect than FIR versus IIR. With 0.6-second FIR features, Mov1 versus Mov0 improves the subject mean by 7.37 percentage points in 6×6 (95% interval 4.55–10.43) and 17.06 points in 3×3 (12.97–21.28). For Mov1 at 0.6 seconds, the paired IIR-minus-FIR difference is −1.10 points in 6×6 (−3.72 to +1.40) and 0.00 in 3×3 (−2.62 to +2.62). These comparisons do not establish a universal winner.

The initial independent test subset scored FIR, 1 second/20 Hz, Mov1 at **161/165 (97.6%)**. It is retained as earlier evidence; the expanded target-labelled evaluation below supersedes its coverage.

## Expanded ground truth evaluation

The user subsequently supplied exact targets or explicit omissions for every 2021–2024 6×6 play session. All 31 added phrases match the recorded attempt counts; punctuation, retries, repeated letters and clear-text keys are retained verbatim. The existing 2025 labels are unchanged. This yields 701 labels in 41 recordings, with nine explicit omissions across the complete 50-recording 6×6 replay pool. No 3×3 accuracy labels were supplied.

Five included 2022 recordings (subjects 06–10) end after three repetitions, contributing 94 letters. The other 36 recordings end after five, contributing 607. All 20 settings use the same attempts; the main endpoint follows the actual recorded protocol. Fixed three/five-cycle strata and cumulative metrics retain their explicit denominators. No additional cycles or targets are inferred.

The frozen within-subject learning-to-play models produce 66,340 scored cumulative predictions and 14,020 final predictions. No test targets enter training. [Scoring evidence](../../../src/_test-output/p300-corpus-comparison-20260925/scored-transfer-all-years/run.json), [target audit](../../../src/_test-output/p300-corpus-comparison-20260925/scored-transfer-all-years/label-audit.json) and [five regression checks](../../../src/_test-output/p300-corpus-comparison-20260925/scored-transfer-all-years/tests.json) include exact preservation of all 16,500 previously scored 2025 decisions.

FIR / 1 second / 20 Hz with Mov1 or Mat2 leads at **658/701 (93.9%)**, versus Mov0 **650/701 (92.7%)** and historical Mat0 **631/701 (90.0%)**. Mov1's five-cycle stratum scores 574/607 (94.6%); its three-cycle stratum scores 84/94 (89.4%). The 1-second IIR Mov1 score is 642/701 (91.6%).

For 1-second FIR, regularization corrects 18 Mov0 errors but introduces ten new errors: 12 recordings improve, 23 tie and six worsen. Its paired subject-mean improvement is +1.07 percentage points (95% interval −0.55 to +2.65). At 0.6-second FIR the gain is larger: 54 net letters, +7.37 points (+4.93 to +9.97). For 1-second Mov1, FIR exceeds IIR by +2.17 subject-mean points (+0.60 to +3.79). Intervals remain exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.
