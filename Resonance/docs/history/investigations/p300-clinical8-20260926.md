## Inputs and retention

Read all 43 `sbj_01`–`sbj_43/Clin_matrix-rec-001.h5` calibration recordings under `Q:/P300Data/data - raw`. No rec-002 recording or play target enters this study. Each recording contains ten 6×6 letters, with five cycles of twelve row/column flashes per letter, at native 1000 Hz. Labels come from the recorded training target and flash membership, checked against the generated sequence and settings.

The supplied [movicom8.ced](../../../dist/venues/MPRAC/montages/movicom8.ced) has exact matches in the 31-channel acquisition: Cz, Pz, C3, C4, PO7, PO8, O1, O2 correspond to source channels 14, 23, 13, 15, 26, 28, 29, 30 (one-based). No position substitutions or rereferencing were required. This replicates electrode selection, not amplifier or acquisition-reference characteristics.

The [separate retained pool](../../../equipment/Movicom/classifier-reference/corpus-clinical8/README.md) contains about 162 MB: exact native eight-channel samples, original photo samples and block timing, messages/settings, training labels and eligibility. All 430 original letters remain available. The other 23 EEG channels and noncalibration recordings are outside this extraction. Every input has one trailing all-zero HDF row unsupported by its block records; only that row was omitted. Original HDF hashes were unchanged before/after extraction, and saved EEG subsets were checked byte-exact on reopening. Reanalysis needs no Q drive or original HDF files.

## Optical alignment and fixed eligibility

Each flash uses its detected optical ON sample, with unique ON/OFF correspondence and duration checks. The original photo is preserved. In 26 recordings, a dark-state code near +640.000061 is mapped to the valid signal's low level for detection only. A 21-sample median and percentile-midpoint threshold define transitions. The [pool reference](../../../equipment/Movicom/classifier-reference/corpus-clinical8/README.md) states all gates and timestamp conventions. This is a threshold-crossing convention, not a measurement of exact first-photon onset.

Five whole letters fail the optical checks: sbj_27 letter 2, sbj_37 letter 7, sbj_38 letter 5, sbj_39 letter 1, sbj_43 letter 1. Missing/ambiguous matches and abnormal pulse duration cause rejection; no EEG amplitude or accuracy criterion is used. All methods share the remaining **425 letters across all 43 recordings**. No software-timestamp fallback is silently substituted.

## Frozen methods

Use within-recording leave-one-whole-letter-out validation. Train on all five cycles of the other eight or nine eligible letters, then predict the held-out letter cumulatively after cycles 1–5. No held-out letter enters fitting. Parameters come from the earlier comparison and [6×6 follow-up winners](p300-followup-20260926.md); none is selected using this new pool.

Cross three fitters with four preprocessing profiles:

- Mov0: original class-centered n−1 covariance, equal class contribution, no regularization; original pivoted QR, normalization and bias.
- Mat0: historical uncentered second moments divided by n, equal class contribution and 1% mean-diagonal ridge. This is not the subsequently changed centered MATLAB default.
- Best: class-centered n−1 covariance pooled by class degrees of freedom, with 0.3% mean-diagonal ridge.

Stock Movicom uses FIR 1–10 Hz, 0–1000 ms and 20 Hz point features. Stock MATLAB uses causal Butterworth bandpass prototype order 4 (total order 8), 1.5–10 Hz, 0–600 ms and 50 Hz point features, matching the earlier comparison profile. Best FIR uses 0.1–15 Hz, 100–600 ms and 50 Hz point features. Best IIR uses separate HP order 2 at 0.3 Hz plus LP order 4 at 12 Hz, 100–600 ms and forward 40 ms means every 20 ms. Both best profiles add the previously fixed causal anti-alias lowpass.

Filtering is continuous and causal, reset once per recording. No filtfilt, epoch-wise filtering, resampling, channel scaling or baseline subtraction. Port FIR lengths to 1000 Hz as `(taps500 − 1) × 2 + 1`: 4093 stock and 2045 best taps. Physical group delay remains 2.046/1.022 seconds; passbands, epoch bounds and feature times remain in seconds. Native-rate coefficients are redesigned, not claimed bit-identical to the 500 Hz filters. Delay-aligned FIR features require later samples; best FIR/IIR data waits are 1.622/0.600 seconds, excluding system processing overhead.

## Results

Correct letters after all five cycles, denominator 425 in every cell:

| Preprocessing | Mov0 | Mat0 | Best |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Stock Movicom | 397 | 400 | 408 |
| Stock MATLAB | 381 | 394 | 409 |
| Best FIR | 393 | 402 | **414** |
| Best IIR | 401 | 399 | **413** |

The complete pipelines score 93.41% for stock Movicom/Mov0, 92.71% for stock MATLAB/Mat0, 97.41% for Best FIR/Best and 97.18% for Best IIR/Best. The best fitter improves every matched preprocessing comparison. FIR versus IIR differs by one correct letter: four letters favour FIR, three favour IIR. The subject-mean IIR-minus-FIR difference is −0.23 percentage points, with paired subject-bootstrap 95% interval −1.16 to +0.70. This pool does not establish an accuracy advantage for either finalist; the IIR's shorter data wait remains useful.

Against stock Movicom, Best FIR has a subject-mean gain of 3.95 points (95% interval 1.63–6.28); against stock MATLAB, 4.73 points (2.51–7.31). Intervals use 20,000 paired subject resamples, seed 9262026, and are descriptive/unadjusted. These evaluate fixed settings in a new acquisition pool, still with within-recording calibration folds; they do not measure separate-recording or prospective online accuracy.
